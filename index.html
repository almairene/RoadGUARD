      How the score works

      Each location score is the highest active report severity (low 20, medium 50, high 80), plus up to 10 points for repeat reports and up to 10 points for recency (reported within 24 hours: 10; within 7 days: 5), capped at 100. Repaired and rejected reports are excluded. This is a transparent demonstration heuristic, not a validated engineering assessment.
